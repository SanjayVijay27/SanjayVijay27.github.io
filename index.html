About Me

Hello! I'm Sanjay Vijay, an undergraduate at Purdue University studying computer science. I'm passionate about artificial intelligence, computer vision, and robotics. I've also recently become interested in competitive programming. At Purdue, I lead the algorithm subteam of our RoboMaster club, where I've improved computer vision and navigation for our autonomous robots and taught new members the skills to do the same. I'm also a researcher at Purdue's Cognitive Robot Autonomy and Learning (CoRAL) Lab, where I'm working on adapting splatting algorithms for dark environments. This semester, I'll be a teaching assistant for CS 18200: Foundations of Computer Science and CS 21100: Competitive Programming I. When it comes to industry experience, I've been a summer intern at Draper under the Autonomy and Real-Time Planning Group twice, working on embedded systems, sensor fusion, and vehicle data analysis. Feel free to reach out if you have any questions!
